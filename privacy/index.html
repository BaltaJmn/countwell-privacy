Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short: Countwell does not collect your content, and has no account, analytics, ads or tracking. Everything you make stays on your iPhone. Purchases are recorded by RevenueCat with an anonymous identifier (see Purchases).

Photos and the camera

Countwell asks for the camera so it can photograph the pills you want to count, and lets you choose a photo from your library through the system photo picker, which only gives the app the photo you pick. Each photo is analysed on your iPhone to find the pills. Photos are never uploaded, never saved by Countwell to your library and are discarded when you close the count.

Your counts

When you save a count, its number, the label you type and the date are stored on your iPhone only. They are not sent anywhere. You can delete any of them in the app, and they are all removed if you delete the app. If you export your history, you choose where the file goes.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Countwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it asks Apple whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

To know, and to restore, whether this installation has Pro, Countwell uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat receives each in-app purchase you make, assigns the installation an anonymous app user ID, and stores that ID, the purchase history (product, date, price and store transaction IDs) and basic technical device data (OS version, app version, country or storefront). Nothing else from Countwell, such as your photos or your counts, is sent to RevenueCat or anywhere else. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

To ask for deletion of the purchase record, write to baltajmn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Countwell purchase data". Apple keeps its own record of the order under its own policies.

What Countwell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The only third-party code in the app is the RevenueCat purchases library, described under Purchases. Countwell works with the network turned off; the purchase record is sent to RevenueCat when the device is online.

Children

Countwell is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, children included. The only thing that goes to RevenueCat is an anonymous installation ID and the purchase record (see Purchases).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: baltajmn@gmail.com. The developer is Baltasar Jiménez Rincón, Spain (details in the legal notice).
