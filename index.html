Countwell

Count pills in one photo

Countwell is a pill counter for iPhone. Spread the tablets or capsules on a plain tray, take one photo and get the count, with a dot on every pill so you can check it at a glance.

No account, no internet. Counting happens on your iPhone. Photos are never uploaded, and Countwell has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking.

How it works

Take a photo or pick one from your library. Countwell finds each pill and marks it. Tap a dot to remove it, or tap a pill it missed to add one. Save the count with a label (drug, bottle, lot) and export your history as a spreadsheet.

Price

Your first 10 counts are free, so you can see whether it works for you. After that, Countwell Pro is a subscription: $24.99 a year or $4.99 a month in the US (prices vary by country). Cancel anytime in your Apple account settings.

For an exact count

Use a plain surface whose colour differs from the pills, spread them so none are stacked, and hold the phone flat above them. Countwell is a counting aid: double-check counts wherever accuracy matters.
